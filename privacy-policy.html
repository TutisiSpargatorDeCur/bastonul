About this policy

This policy explains how Bastonul handles information when you visit this website, contact us, or use BastoBrowser. The browser and website have different data practices, described separately below.

Website use

This website is intended to provide information about Bastonul and its products. Bastonul does not intentionally ask you to provide personal information to browse these pages. Our hosting provider may receive basic technical information, such as an IP address and request details, when serving the site; how that information is handled depends on the provider.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond and handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary. You can contact us at dickergol@gmail.com.

BastoBrowser

BastoBrowser stores browser settings and browsing data, such as history, bookmarks, cookies, and site data, locally on your device as part of normal browser operation. Your visits to websites and searches are handled by the websites and search provider you choose, under their own privacy policies. Browser updates may contact the configured release service to check for a newer version.

Third-party websites and services

Links on this site may take you to third-party services, including download, search, or other websites. Those services have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information with them; Bastonul does not control their handling of your data.

Retention and your choices

Emails are retained only as needed to respond and maintain relevant records. Browser data can be managed or deleted using the browser’s settings and your device’s controls. You can stop using this website or software at any time.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our products or practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions, email dickergol@gmail.com.
